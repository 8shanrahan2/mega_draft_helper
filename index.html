Why the model behaves this way

Draft objectives

Mega Draft archetypesInfer a probability mix across beatdown, control, cycle pressure, bridge pressure, bait, siege, graveyard control, split-lane pressure, air beatdown, and counterpush instead of importing ladder deck labels. Specialized archetypes require their defining card or pressure package.
Deck structureScore the elixir curve, cheap-cycle count, minimum four-card cycle cost, threat-conditioned swarm clear, heavy-card saturation, and repeated-role redundancy relative to the inferred strategy.
Champion / Hero slotTrack the max-one Champion slot separately. An open slot gives a Champion only a small +0.8 tiebreaker; finishing without one is not penalized.
Counter coverageReward answers to threats the opponent has already revealed. Spell counters distinguish fragile swarms, medium swarms, spawners, and merely multi-unit troops instead of treating all “swarm” tags alike.
Counter denialOnce a win condition is committed, sharply increase the value of removing its strongest remaining answers from the shared pool.
Scarcity & optionalityIncrease urgency when important roles are disappearing while still favoring flexible early picks before the draft has revealed enough information.
Patch adjustmentUse temporary Aug 26 priors until enough post-patch Mega Draft games overwrite stale 3-day data.
